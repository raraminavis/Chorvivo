Why we’re building this

A dance lives in the bodies that made it.

It gets made in a room, over an afternoon. By the next rehearsal half of it is gone, or it’s forty videos deep in somebody’s camera roll.

Music has scores. Film has scripts. Dance has had memory, and whoever happened to be filming.

DoDance keeps the record while you work: each take and each note, in order, and yours.

FAQ

What if there’s no music?+

File takes by hand, or turn sorting off.

Can I slow a take down?+

Yes. 1×, .75×, .5× or .25×, on loop.

Who can see my footage?+

Only you, until you share it.

Who is the beta for?+

Choreographers and studios.

What does it cost?+

One plan with everything in it. We’re still setting the price, and beta members will hear it first.
