How it works

  1. Record the room

    Capture rehearsal video straight into the work, phone in hand. Rough takes are the point — nothing needs to be clean.

  2. Mark what changed

    Note the counts you reworked and who was in the room. Every take stays in order, so you can find the version from three weeks ago.

  3. Leave with the record

    Your process becomes a dated, exportable log of what you made and when — ready if you ever need to show your work is yours.

Who it's for

Freelance and early-career commercial choreographers — music video, K-pop, tour, and brand work.

If your archive is a camera roll, a few Drive folders, and clips someone AirDropped you after a shoot, this is built for you.
